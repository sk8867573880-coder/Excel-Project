Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported? ▼
We support standard Microsoft Excel files (.xlsx, .xls), comma-separated values (.csv), and tab-delimited text files (.tsv). Multi-sheet Excel files will prompt you with a clean sheet selector upon upload.
Do I need to know Python, R, or SQL? ▼
No. Excel2Dashboard is specifically designed to eliminate the steep learning curves of complex BI authoring tools. The system determines appropriate charts and aggregations automatically.
Is my data secure? ▼
Yes. Files are processed locally in your browser. We never store your raw data on our servers permanently. All analytics are performed transparently in-memory during your session.
Can I export my dashboards? ▼
Absolutely. Export high-resolution PNG images, vector SVGs, or PDF summary sheets that you can use in presentations, journal submissions, or team reports.
What if I have missing or incomplete data? ▼
The platform automatically identifies missing values, empty cells, and anomalies. You'll get a data quality report so you understand what's being worked with.
Can I filter and explore the data? ▼
Yes. Filter across multiple dimensions simultaneously to focus on specific cohorts, time windows, or outlier groups. Interactive charts respond to your selections in real-time.
Is there a limit on dataset size? ▼
The browser-based approach handles datasets from a few hundred to hundreds of thousands of rows efficiently. Very large datasets (millions of rows) may require data pre-filtering.
How do I get support? ▼
Contact our support team through the platform, or email support@excel2dashboard.com. For clinical institutions, we offer dedicated onboarding and training.
Can I customize the dashboard layout? ▼
Yes. Adjust visual layout, prioritize key metrics, toggle alternate chart renderings, and select color palettes that match your institution's branding.
Is there a free trial or pilot program? ▼
Yes! We offer a free audit for ₹1 to help you understand how Excel2Dashboard can transform your research workflow. No credit card required to start.
